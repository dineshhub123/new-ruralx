Ruralx Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 09 Feb 2026

1. Introduction

Ruralx values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our mobile application, website, and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Delivery address and location details
  • Order and transaction history
  • Device information and app usage data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our app and services
  • To send order updates and important notifications

4. Payment Information

Ruralx does not store your card or payment details. Payments are processed securely through trusted third-party payment providers.

5. Data Sharing

We may share your data only with:

  • Delivery partners for order fulfillment
  • Payment gateway providers
  • Legal authorities if required by law

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze traffic, and enhance app performance.

8. Your Rights

  • You can request access to your personal data
  • You can request correction of incorrect information
  • You can request account deletion

9. Children's Privacy

Ruralx services are not intended for children under 18 years without parental supervision.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform means you accept updated policies.
